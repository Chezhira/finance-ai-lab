# Finance Dashboards & KPI Pack

A finance analytics concept for turning accounting and operational data into decision-ready dashboards, KPI trees, board packs, and monthly performance commentary.

## Business problem

Many finance teams produce reports but not decision-ready insight.

Common gaps include:

- P&L reports without drivers
- dashboards disconnected from operations
- cash flow visibility arriving too late
- weak variance commentary
- KPIs not linked to controllable actions
- board packs rebuilt manually every month

## What the pack would do

- define KPI trees by business model
- link finance metrics to operational drivers
- create monthly dashboard templates
- produce variance commentary prompts
- support board-level reporting
- track profitability, working capital, and cash flow

## Skills demonstrated
