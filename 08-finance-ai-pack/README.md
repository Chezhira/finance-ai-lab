# Finance AI Pack

A practical finance automation pack for month-end close, bank reconciliation, VAT checks, reporting controls, and finance workflow automation.

## Business problem

Finance teams often operate with a mix of ERP exports, spreadsheets, bank files, tax schedules, and manual reconciliations.

The problem is not only that work is manual. The bigger issue is that finance teams need repeatable, controlled, audit-ready workflows that reduce errors and make month-end faster.

Common issues include:

- manual bank reconciliations
- VAT and tax control gaps
- weak month-end close visibility
- repeated spreadsheet preparation
- inconsistent evidence packs
- poor exception tracking
- finance data scattered across systems
