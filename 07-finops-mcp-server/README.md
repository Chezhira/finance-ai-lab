# FinOps MCP Server

A finance operations MCP server concept for connecting finance workflows, ERP data, reporting controls, reconciliations, and AI-assisted analysis through a structured tool layer.

## Business problem

Finance teams are increasingly expected to use AI, but most finance data lives inside ERP systems, spreadsheets, bank files, tax schedules, and reporting packs.

Without a controlled access layer, AI workflows can become risky, inconsistent, or disconnected from the real finance process.

Common issues include:

- finance data scattered across systems and files
- AI tools lacking safe access to structured finance context
- manual extraction from ERP systems
- weak control over what AI can query or process
- no clear bridge between finance workflows and automation
- finance teams needing practical AI without losing auditability

## What the project demonstrates

- MCP/server-based architecture for finance operations
- controlled access to finance workflows and datasets
- AI-ready finance tooling concepts
- bridge between ERP data and AI assistants
- structured tool design for finance teams
- automation of finance queries, reconciliations, and reporting checks
