# Audit Evidence Pack Generator

A structured finance automation concept for producing audit-ready packs from reconciliations, exception reports, close checklists, and supporting schedules.

## Business problem

Audit support is often scattered across:

- spreadsheets
- ERP exports
- emails
- PDFs
- bank statements
- reconciliations
- inventory schedules
- tax schedules
- manual explanations

This makes audit response slow, inconsistent, and stressful.

## What the generator would do

- organize monthly close evidence by area
- package reconciliations and supporting schedules
- identify missing evidence
- create exception logs
- support preparer/reviewer sign-off
- generate audit-ready folders or reports
- create a clean index for auditors and management

## Skills demonstrated

- audit-readiness automation
